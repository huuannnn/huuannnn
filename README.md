## `> about-me`

I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science and a Teaching Assistant. My research lies at the intersection of graph learning, temporal modeling, and intelligent transportation systems.

I focus on developing lightweight and effective neural architectures that can capture complex spatial relationships and evolving temporal patterns. In particular, I am interested in using graph and hypergraph structures to improve forecasting and reasoning over dynamic real-world data.

- Currently developing graph and hypergraph models for traffic forecasting
- Exploring temporal reasoning and knowledge graph completion
- Open to research collaborations in graph learning and spatio-temporal modeling
